# Fee accounting specification

There are three potentially distinct fees: Uniswap protocol fee (controlled by v4 governance), native LP fee (Fayn's dynamic schedule), and hook fee (zero in Fayn). Fayn protocol revenue is zero. Hook cannot take or redistribute manager fees. No 80/15/5 allocation is implemented. Native LP fees belong to active position liquidity, following v4 fee growth and integer rounding. If a protocol fee is enabled, v4 deducts it according to core mechanics; do not assume all combined fees belong to LPs. Local PoolManager has no protocol fee configured.

Worked one-step illustration, ignoring price impact: a gross input of 100 tokens at 3% with zero protocol fee leaves approximately 97 tokens for price movement and 3 tokens as LP fees. This is not a quote; actual v4 step math rounds up and fee growth truncates dust. At halfway through a 30-minute 3% → 0.3% schedule the LP fee is 1.65% (16,500 millionths). Opening and terminal timestamps are tested. A raw input of 101 at a 3% fee may pay 4 raw units due to rounding in the appropriate single swap step. Exact-output inputs are grossed up by v4; Fayn does not approximate them for execution.

Manager receives exactly the negative caller deltas via sync, SafeERC20 transferFrom and settle. Router requires actual settled amount equal debt. Positive deltas go directly to the wallet through take. Router residual asset balance must remain zero in tests. For a position action, `callerDelta` already includes principal and fees. `feesAccrued` is informational and must not be added again to `callerDelta`. PositionChanged emits both explicitly; the UI's collectible earnings are the result of eth_call simulation of modifyLiquidity(change=0), never summed swap fees. Fee growth rounding can leave uncollectible dust. Donations can inflate fee growth, so collected fees are not proof of trading revenue or profitability.

Every isolated swap test checks `wallet delta + manager delta = 0` in each token. Randomized alternating swaps assert wallet + manager conservation and zero router balances. Fee collection followed by immediate re-collection yields zero. LP withdrawal burns only owned liquidity units; min withdrawal bounds and max deposit bounds are enforced independently. Collected fees are included in the wallet transfer, not separately minted or credited.

A sponsor deposit of 12.5 tUSDC creates exactly 12,500,000 raw units of vault liability. Contract checks token balance increase equals requested funding. Claim zeroes available amount and decreases liability before transfer. It cannot create an unfunded entitlement; repeated claim without new funding reverts. Vault balance equals outstanding liability for supported standard tokens, absent unsolicited transfers. Extra unsolicited donations do not create claimable grants and cannot be reclaimed. This is separate sponsor spending; it is not LP fee redistribution. No real rebates should be enabled based on the synthetic farming experiment.

Do not label collected fees as total LP return. Principal changes with inventory composition, market price and impermanent loss; gas further affects outcomes. This app does not provide a price oracle or total-return metric.
