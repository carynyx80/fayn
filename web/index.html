Know the terms.

Protection model

Opening fees are intended to decline to a normal fee on a published schedule. Fees apply consistently and do not identify humans or bots. This model cannot guarantee protection against sandwiches, arbitrage or losses.

Fees and rewards

The current contracts use native Uniswap v4 LP fee accounting. No Fayn hook or protocol fee is implemented. Trader rebates remain disabled for production. No yield or allocation is promised.

Deployment and security

No Fayn contracts are deployed on mainnet. The contracts have not received an independent audit. Wallet connection does not enable transactions or prove wallet ownership through a signature.

Wallet connection

Scan the connection QR using Robinhood Wallet. Only account and network connection permissions are requested. No approval, signature, deposit or transfer request is supported by this release.
